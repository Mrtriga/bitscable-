Beyond keywords

Putting "plumber" on a plumber's website doesn't make it an SEO strategy.

Search intent matters. Different searches indicate different needs and levels of commercial intent.

Local

"plumber near me"

Informational

"how to fix a leaking tap"

Commercial · Local

"emergency plumber Atlanta GA"

We structure pages around what the visitor needs to understand — not simply how many times a keyword can appear.

Setting expectations

SEO isn't instant.

  • Search engines need time to discover and process changes.
  • Competitive searches can take longer to move.
  • Technical fixes remove obstacles — they don't guarantee rankings.
  • Rankings depend on competition, content, authority and search behavior.
  • SEO requires ongoing measurement and iteration, not a one-time setup.

We can improve the things your website controls. We won't promise what search engines don't.
