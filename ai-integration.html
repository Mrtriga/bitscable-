Responsible AI

Smart Integration Needs Human Oversight.

  • AI output can be wrong
  • Important information should be reviewed
  • Sensitive information must be handled carefully
  • AI should not automatically make critical business decisions without appropriate controls
  • Businesses should understand what data is being sent to third-party AI services
  • Human escalation should exist where necessary

The goal isn't to remove people from the process. It's to remove unnecessary work from the process.
