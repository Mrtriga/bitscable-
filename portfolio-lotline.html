Lotline

A real estate listings site with property search, detail pages, and an inquiry-focused layout.

View Live Demo

The Brief

A real estate operation needed a listings site that presents properties clearly and gets serious inquiries moving, without burying visitors in noise.

What We Built

  • A property listings grid with search/filtering
  • Individual property detail pages
  • Clear inquiry calls-to-action throughout
  • A mobile-first responsive layout

Why It Matters

Real estate visitors are comparison-shopping across many listings at once — this build keeps that browsing fast and keeps the path to "contact about this property" always one click away.
