Common questions

Can you maintain a website you didn't build?

Yes, subject to being able to access and work with its existing technology.

Do I need a maintenance plan?

Not necessarily. The right approach depends on how often the website changes and how technically complex it is.

Can you update text and images?

Yes.

Can you fix a broken website?

Yes, where the issue is within the website and the systems we can access. We'll identify external dependencies when relevant.

Can you maintain WordPress websites?

Yes, where appropriate access is available.

Will maintenance improve my SEO?

Maintenance can protect technical SEO health, but maintenance itself isn't a substitute for a dedicated SEO strategy.

Can you tell me if my website needs a redesign instead?

Yes. If maintenance would simply keep an outdated experience alive, we'll say so.
