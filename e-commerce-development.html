Common questions

Can I sell online without a full backend?

Yes — many small catalogs run fine on a static front-end paired with a hosted checkout provider; we'll walk you through free-tier options based on what you're selling.

Do you handle payment processing?

We integrate with your chosen provider (e.g. Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe) — the account and fees stay directly with you and the provider, we handle the integration.

How long does it take?

Typically 1–3 weeks, depending on catalog size and complexity.

What if my catalog grows later?

We build with room to grow — starting static-first keeps costs low now, and we can add backend inventory management later without a full rebuild.

Do you handle product photos or copywriting?

Not directly, but we'll work with what you provide, or point you to affordable options — the build itself focuses on structure and flow.

Ready to sell online?

Tell us what you're selling — we'll show you what a store built around it looks like.
