Common questions

How long does a custom build take vs. a template?

A professionally customized template can often launch in 1–2 weeks. A fully custom build typically takes longer, depending on the number of pages and features involved — we'll give you a clear timeline once we understand your scope.

Can I start with a template and upgrade to custom later?

Yes — many businesses launch faster with a template and evolve toward more custom features as they grow. We build with that flexibility in mind from the start.

Do I need to know what I want before we start?

No — that's what the discovery phase is for. We'll ask the right questions to figure out what your website actually needs to do.

Will my website work well on mobile?

Yes, every build is responsive by default — designed and tested across phone, tablet, and desktop screens before launch.

What happens after the website launches?

Your website doesn't have to stay static. We can add new pages, features, e-commerce capability, or SEO improvements as your business grows.
